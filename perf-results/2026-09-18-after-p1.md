# Performance After P1 — 2026-09-18

## Environment

- Machine: MacBook (Apple Silicon)
- Node: v22.18.0
- pnpm: 10.23.0
- vitest: v4.1.11

## P1 fix

Hoisted `cloneDeep(feature)` + `recursiveJSONParse` from `evalExpression` (once per expression)
to `evalSimpleLayerFeature` (once per feature). All expressions for a given feature now share
one pre-computed parsed copy.

Changed file: `src/mantle/evaluator/simple/index.ts`

## Evaluator benchmark (`pnpm bench`)

| Case                    | Before (ms) | After (ms) | Speedup |
|-------------------------|-------------|------------|---------|
| 1k  features × 5  expr  |        6.40 |       2.89 |   2.2×  |
| 10k features × 5  expr  |       66.64 |      29.95 |   2.2×  |
| 10k features × 10 expr  |      117.69 |      38.42 |   3.1×  |
| 10k features × 15 expr  |      175.54 |      46.99 |   3.7×  |
| 35k features × 10 expr  |      417.40 |     138.80 |   3.0×  |

### Scaling after fix

| Ratio                              | Before | After |
|------------------------------------|--------|-------|
| mean(10k × 15) / mean(10k × 5)    |  2.63× | 1.57× |
| mean(10k × 10) / mean(10k × 5)    |  1.77× | 1.28× |

After the fix, ratios no longer track linearly with expression count — the remaining scaling
is the cost of evaluating more expressions, not clone overhead.

## Notes

- Speedup scales with expression count: 5-expr cases gain ~2.2×, 15-expr cases gain ~3.7×
- This matches theory: saving (exprCount - 1) clones per feature means higher-expr cases benefit more
- 35k × 10: 417ms → 139ms — the highest-impact real-world case
- All 198 tests pass after the change
